How to read this list

“Check-in confirmed” means Swarm names the artist or a venue/date visit matches a named event in another source. “Photo corroborated” means inspected concert imagery matches the date and venue. “Attendee confirmed” means Tal remembers attending; its notes record any evidence that conflicts. Tickets and calendar events alone establish a booking or plan, not attendance.
