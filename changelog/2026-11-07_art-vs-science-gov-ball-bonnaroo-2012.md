## Learned

- **Bonnaroo 2012 was missing entirely.** Tal bought a resale wristband from Craigslist on Jun 4, registered it and drove to Manchester, TN. They left before Phish closed on Sunday.
- **Art vs. Science at Bonnaroo:** Sat Jun 9, a 30-minute afternoon set on the Sonic Stage, then a midnight set at the Great Taste Lounge. The entry is dated Jun 9 for that reason.
- **Art vs. Science at Gov Ball 2012:** Sat Jun 23, Hype Machine Stage, about 12:15–12:45 PM. That's the day before the Brooklyn Bowl check-in.
- **Gov Ball 2012 has no ticket or check-in evidence.** In April 2012 Tal told a friend they might go "day 2 only" if tickets were cheap. The memory is the only proof.
- **The Graffiti6 show (2012-06-07, Highline Ballroom) was probably not attended.** On Jun 4 Tal emailed "Free tix to Graffiti6", giving the tickets away because of Bonnaroo.
- Google Calendar has no Bonnaroo or Gov Ball 2012 events.
