# The Kills at Pier 63: Tal's recollection

Tal has a vivid memory of seeing The Kills at a park on the West Side over 10 years ago. The show was already in the archive, so no new row was needed.

## Learned / corrected

- The memory matches the free Converse City Carnage show at Pier 63. It is the only West Side park Kills show in the archive; the other four Kills rows are indoor venues (Bowery Ballroom 2014, Warsaw 2016, Music Hall of Williamsburg 2016, Webster Hall 2024).
- Existing evidence: Ticketmaster order email (2012-06-22), Bowery Presents announcement that Tal forwarded the same day, and two photos taken at 20:38 and 20:39 on the night.
- One web result links a QRO photo gallery to a Pier 63 show on August 23, 2012. The date stays August 18, which the announcement email subject, the flyer and the photo timestamps all support.
- Tal did not name the pier or the year, so the match to this row is inferred from the artist, the location and the time frame.
