## What was learned

- setlist.fm lists eleven In Flames shows at the Palladium between 1999 and 2014. Those in a plausible range for Tal: Apr 25, 2008 (New England Metal and Hardcore Festival X), Dec 6, 2008, Feb 17, 2012 (with Trivium), and Dec 19, 2014 (co-headline with Opeth).
- Feb 17, 2012 is ruled out: Tal has a Swarm check-in at Sleigh Bells, Terminal 5, New York, that night. The Ticketmaster alert Tal got for that tour (Nov 10, 2011) was for the Best Buy Theater date in New York on Feb 18.
- Tal's other 2006-2008 shows are in Colorado, so the 2008 Worcester dates are less likely. Dec 19, 2014 is the best fit, but Tal has not confirmed it yet, so the status is `Needs review`.
- Gmail: no ticket order for any Worcester In Flames show; only Ticketmaster performer alerts (Mar 2011, Nov 2011) and 2005-2006 Killswitch Engage / In Flames fan-presale emails for other dates. Google Calendar: only the 2006 Colorado Springs In Flames show. Google Photos: no results for "Worcester".
- Ghost Cult's review of the night says In Flames played before Opeth and Opeth played no encore.
