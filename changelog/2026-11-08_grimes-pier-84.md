## What was learned

- Tal recalled seeing The Flaming Lips and Grimes at a West Side pier near the Intrepid, and was unsure whether it was one show.
- They were separate events on consecutive nights, a short walk apart:

| Date | Show | Place | Tal's attendance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2012-08-09 | Grimes, Wild Nothing, DIIV (River Rocks, free) | Pier 84 | Attended, per Gchats that day and the next |
| 2012-08-10 | The Flaming Lips, Grizzly Bear, Santigold, fun. (Colbchella) | USS Intrepid, Pier 86 | Apparently missed, per a 2012-08-14 Gchat |

- The Flaming Lips were not logged for 2012-08-10. Tal's chat doesn't name the band, but it describes missing Colbert's festival on the Intrepid.
- Tal later recalled Santigold and DIIV as well. DIIV fits Pier 84, and Santigold was on the Colbchella bill.
- Santigold was also on the bill on days Tal attended Bonnaroo (2012-06-09) and Governors Ball (2012-06-23), so that memory may come from one of those.
- After seeing the conflict, Tal chose to leave Colbchella out of the archive.
- Tal's other logged Flaming Lips show is GoogaMooga on 2013-05-17.
