## What was learned

- Tal's message (2026-11-10) is the attendance source for all three.
- Keystone: a Craigslist reply to Tal on 2008-10-30 said the Ogden ticket was sold and that Girl Talk was "playing a free show in Keystone on DEC 5th". 5280's listing from Dec 5, 2008 puts it in Keystone's Friday Night Lights series at River Run Village, 8 p.m., free. setlist.fm files the same date under "Keystone Conference Center, Dillon, CO"; the 5280 venue was used and the conflict kept in `notes`.
- Jungle: the Tumblr IRL announcement, Splash RSVP page and flyer agree on Sat Jun 21, 2014, 8 PM, The Wick, 260 Meserole St, Brooklyn, free and all ages, with visuals by Oliver Hadlee Pearch. No other acts were billed. setlist.fm has no Jun 21 entry (it has Jun 18 Bowery Ballroom, Jun 19 Union Transfer, Jun 20 WXPN).
