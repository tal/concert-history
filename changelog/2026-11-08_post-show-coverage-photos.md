# Post-show coverage photos

Searched all 236 published concert records for post-show photo coverage using each artist, date and venue. Added 176 verified, hotlinked photographs to 49 gigs. Another 159 records have no usable exact-show match from this pass; 28 have unavailable candidate sources. Each published record now has `coverage_research` with the query, research date, reviewed sources, outcome and matching/rejection notes. Excluded records were not published or modified by this batch.

Jack White’s official live-photo archive supplied 19 Radio City Music Hall photos from September 30, 2012, and 43 Roseland Ballroom photos from May 21, 2012. Both official galleries credit Jo McCaughey. Other sources include BrooklynVegan, We All Want Someone To Shout For, Stereogum, Time Out New York, QRO, NYCTaper, WFUV, music publications and photographers’ own reports. Named credits are preserved; where a publication does not identify the photographer, the credit explicitly says so. Images remain hosted by their original providers.

Personal galleries are labeled “Tal’s photos.” Published images appear in a separate “Coverage photos” gallery with visible credits, captions, photographed dates and links to the reports. The lightbox keeps the attribution and offers “Read coverage.” Timeline thumbnails distinguish coverage, and the photo filter includes both kinds. JSON/catalog exports preserve separate arrays; Markdown counts personal and coverage images separately.

Rejected nearby-night and uncertain images include Glass Animals at Brooklyn Mirage on August 8 rather than August 9, 2022; Emei at Mercury Lounge on November 4 rather than November 5, 2023; and galleries combining several nights without identifying the photographed night. Refused, removed and challenged sources were left alone. No web coverage was used as attendance evidence.

The merge script updates the latest YAML by concert ID and verifies artist/date/venue before applying additions. A structured comparison confirmed all existing concert fields, personal photo references, statuses and 279 personal photos were unchanged.

Validation: build and typecheck passed; five provenance tests passed, including wrong-night rejection, festival-day matching, attribution, URL safety and research consistency. Original image URLs returned image content successfully for all 176 retained photographs. HTTP checks verified the rendered galleries/credits on all 49 gig pages and coverage labels/filter attributes on all 49 timeline rows. Interactive visual checking was unavailable because the browser access host was unavailable. Existing build warnings remain for missing artist metadata (Wild Nothing) and 115 incomplete support/setlist/poster searches; these are separate from the completed coverage-photo search.
