# R.E.M. at Red Rocks, June 3, 2008

Tal remembered seeing R.E.M. at Red Rocks sometime in 2007–2009. Email pins it to the Accelerate tour stop on Tuesday, June 3, 2008, and Tal confirmed it.

## Added

| Concert | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `2008-06-03-rem`: R.E.M. with The National and Modest Mouse, Red Rocks Amphitheatre | Attendee confirmed | Forwarded Ticketmaster order (April 5, 2008) for 2 tickets to the June 3 show; a "we got REM tickets" email; Tal's night-of Tumblr post titled "Red Rocks" sent 8:26pm MDT |

New artists: R.E.M., Modest Mouse and The National (exact Spotify matches, Wikipedia descriptions). Red Rocks was already a venue.

## Web research

- **Support:** JamBase's tour table lists Modest Mouse and The National on all dates. Westword's review gives the order (The National first).
- **Setlist:** setlist.fm, 27 songs, with the encore from "Supernatural Superserious". The openers' setlist.fm pages are recorded as support sources.
- **Poster:** none. remhq.com's limited-edition Accelerate posters cover European dates only.
- **Tal's photos:** Google Photos has nothing dated 2008 (searches for the date, month, year and "Red Rocks"), so none are attached.
- **Coverage photos:** unavailable. Marquee Magazine ran an "R.E.M. @ Red Rocks" gallery of this exact night (Soren McCarty), but its image files now 404 and have no archived copy. tour.remhq.com's tour-date page is blocked by robots.txt and was not fetched.

## Corrected or learned

- Westword's review header says "Tuesday, May 30, 2008". May 30 was a Friday, and the review's setlist matches June 3, so the header's day of month is wrong.
