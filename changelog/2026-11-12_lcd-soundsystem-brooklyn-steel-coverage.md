# Coverage photo for LCD Soundsystem at Brooklyn Steel, Apr 10 2017

Tal doubted that no coverage existed for `2017-04-10-lcd-soundsystem`. The 2026-11-08 pass ran one search and recorded `none_found`. A closer look found BrooklynVegan's Night 4 post.

## Changes

- `coverage_photos`: added one hotlinked BrooklynVegan image (`lcd-night-4.jpeg`). It shows the lighting desk with the clock at 21:36 and that night's setlist propped on a stuffed bear. The credit is "Uncredited" because the post doesn't name the photographer.

## What was learned

- BrooklynVegan's [Night 4 post](https://www.brooklynvegan.com/lcd-soundsystem-continued-their-brooklyn-steel-run-night-4-pics-setlist/) (published 2017-04-11) covers Monday 4/10 and prints the full setlist. It agrees with the entry's setlist.fm list.
- The page itself now returns a browser challenge to a plain `concert-history-site/1.0` client. I didn't use a browser to get around that. BrooklynVegan's public WordPress API (`/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?slug=…` and `/wp-json/wp/v2/media/<id>`) answered the plain client normally. The image on `media.brooklynvegan.com` loads normally too.
- The image is the post's only hosted image. The setlist sheet in it goes Movement → Someone Great with no "Yeah", which matches 4/10 and rules out 4/8.
- The post's other "pics" are embedded fan Instagram posts tagged Monday 4/10 (katarina_martinez, trippedout, thedrussell, djchroma, andrewjpeerless and others). Instagram image URLs expire, so they can't be hotlinked and weren't attached. None of them is the source of the lighting-desk photo.
- Rejected: Stereogum's 4/8 "Haircut" story (night 2, with a 2016 Getty photo), The AU Review (night 5, 4/11), BrooklynVegan's night 1, 2 and 5 posts, Atwood Magazine (June 19 residency), and the venue publicist's run announcement (no photos).
